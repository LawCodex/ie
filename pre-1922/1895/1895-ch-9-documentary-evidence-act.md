# Documentary Evidence Act, 1895

CHAPTER IX.

An Act to apply the Documentary Evidence Acts to the Board of Agriculture. [14th May 1895.]

### 1. Application of Documentary Evidence Acts to Board of Agriculture. [See note next page.] 31 & 32 Vict. c. 37. 45 & 46 Vict. c. 9.

**1.** The Documentary Evidence Act, 1868, as amended by the Documentary Evidence Act, 1882, shall apply to the [<sup>2</sup>] Board of Agriculture in like manner as if that Board were mentioned in the first column of the schedule to the first-mentioned Act, and the President or any member of the Board, or the Secretary of the Board, or any person authorised by the President to act on behalf of the Secretary of the Board, were mentioned in the second column of that schedule, and as if the regulations referred to in those Acts included any document issued by the Board.

### 2. Short title.

**2.** This Act may be cited as the Documentary Evidence Act, 1895, and the Documentary Evidence Act, 1868, and the Documentary Evidence Act, 1882, and this Act may be cited collectively as the Documentary Evidence Acts, 1868 to 1895.

[<sup>2</sup> Now styled the Board of Agriculture and Fisheries, *see* 3 Edw. 7. c. 31. s. 1 (1).]
