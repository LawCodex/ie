# Purchase of Land (Ireland) Amendment Act, 1895, Session 2

CHAPTER IV.

An Act to re-enact Section Thirteen of the Purchase of Land (Ireland) Act, 1891. [5th September 1895.]

### 1. Re-enactment with modification of 54 & 55 Vict. c. 48. s. 13.

**1.** Section Thirteen of the Purchase of Land (Ireland) Act, 1891, shall have effect as if enacted in this Act, with the modification that agreements thereunder may be entered into at any time within six months of the passing of this Act.

### 2. Construction and short title.

**2.** This Act shall be construed as one with the Purchase of Land (Ireland) Act, 1891, and may be cited as the Purchase of Land (Ireland) Amendment Act, 1895, Session 2.
