# Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1895

CHAPTER XXX

An Act to amend the Industrial and Provident Societies Act, 1893. [6th July 1895.]

### 1. Short title. 56 & 57 Vict. c. 39. 57 & 58 Vict. c. 8.

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1895. This Act and the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 and 1894, may be cited together as the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 to 1895.

### 2. Proceedings in Scotland.

**2.** In all proceedings in winding up a society in Scotland under the Industrial and Provident Societies Act, 1893, the court having jurisdiction shall be the sheriff court.

[*S*. 3 *substitutes a new subsection for* 56 *&* 57 *Vict. c*. 39. *s*. 7 (1), *see that Act, and s*. 4 *provides for the reprinting of that Act with that substitution*.]
