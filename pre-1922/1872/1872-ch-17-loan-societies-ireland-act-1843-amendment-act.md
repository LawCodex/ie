# Loan Societies (Ireland) Act, 1843, Amendment Act, 1872

CHAPTER XVII.

An Act to amend the Laws for the Regulation of Charitable Loan Societies in Ireland. [27th June 1872.]

[*Preamble recites 6 *&* 7 Vict. c. 91, in this Act referred to as the principal Act*.]

### 1. Short title.

**1.** This Act may be cited as “The Loan Societies (Ireland) Act, 1843, Amendment Act, 1872.”

### 2. Price of forms of promissory note.

**2.** The sum to be paid by each loan society established under the principal Act for every form of a note supplied to them by the Loan Fund Board in pursuance of section forty-six of the principal Act, shall be twopence instead of one penny, as prescribed by the said section.
