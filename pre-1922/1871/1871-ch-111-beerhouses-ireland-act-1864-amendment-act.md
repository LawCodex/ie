# Beerhouses (Ireland) Act (1864) Amendment Act, 1871

CHAPTER CXI.

An Act to amend the Beerhouses (Ireland) Act, 1864, and for other purposes relating thereto. [21st August 1871.]

[*Preamble recites 27 & 28 Vict. c. 35*.]

### 1. Short title.

**1.** This Act may be cited as “The Beerhouses (Ireland) Act (1864) Amendment Act, 1871.”

### 2. Extent.

**2.** This Act shall not apply to England or Scotland.

### 3. Definition of “beer.”

**3.** In the construction of the principal Act the term “beer” shall include ale and porter.

### 4. Extent of repeal of 27 & 28 Vict. c. 35. s. 14.

**4.** [*Repeal of* 27 *&* 28 *Vict. c*. 35. *s*. 14]. Such repeal shall not extend or apply to any person dealing in or selling coffee, tea, cocoa nuts, chocolate, or pepper, and having a license to retail spirits in any quantity not exceeding two quarts at any one time to be consumed elsewhere than on the premises where sold.

[*S*. 5 *rep*. 46 *&* 47 *Vict. c*. 39. (*S.L.R*.)]
