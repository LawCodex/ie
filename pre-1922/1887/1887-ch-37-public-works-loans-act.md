# Public Works Loans Act, 1887

CHAPTER XXXVII.

An Act to grant money for the purpose of certain Local Loans; and for other purposes relating to Local Loans. [16th September 1887.]

### 1. Short title.

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Public Works Loans Act, 1887.

[*Ss*. 2, 3 *rep*. 8 *Edw*. 7. *c*. 49 (*S.L.R*.).]

### 4. Power for certain local authorities to guarantee harbour loans. 38 & 39 Vict. c. 55. 30 & 31 Vict. c. 101. 41 & 42 Vict. c. 52.

**4.** [*Preamble recites* 45 & 46 *Vict. c*. 62, *s*. 7.] The expression “rating authority” as defined in that section shall include:—

(1) As regards England:—

(*a*) any authority being a rural sanitary authority under the Public Health Act, 1875, and the Acts amending the same; and

(*b*) . . . . any representative county body created by Act of Parliament [<sup>1</sup>] .

(2) As regards Scotland:—

(*a*) any local authority under the Public Health (Scotland) Act, 1867, and the Acts amending the same [<sup>2</sup>] ; and

(*b*) . . . . any representative county body created by Act of Parliament.[<sup>3</sup>]

(3) As regards Ireland:—

(*a*) any authority being a rural sanitary authority under the Public Health (Ireland) Act, 1878, and the Acts amending the same; and

(*b*) . . . . any representative county body created by Act of Parliament.[<sup>4</sup>]

[*S*. 5 *rep*. 61 & 62 *Vict. c*. 44, *s*. 8.]

[*Sched. rep*. 8 *Edw*. 7. *c*. 49 (*S.L.R*.).]
