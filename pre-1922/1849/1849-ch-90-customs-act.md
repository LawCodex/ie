# Customs Act, 1849

CHAPTER XC.

An Act to amend the Laws relating to the Customs. [1st August 1849.]

[*Whole Act except ss.* 41-43 *rep.* 16 & 17 *Vict. c.* 107 *s.* 358. *S.* 41 *rep.* 38 & 39 *Vict. c.* 66 (*S.L.R.*). *S.* 42 *rep.* 57 & 58 *Vict. c.* clxxxvii.]

### 43. Goods brought into docks under local Acts.

**43.** And whereas by certain local Acts for establishing or regulating docks officers appointed under such Acts respectively are empowered to cause goods brought into such docks to be landed and warehoused as therein mentioned: Be it enacted, that no goods which shall be brought into any such docks shall be landed and warehoused without due entry thereof.
