# National School Teachers Amendment (Ireland) Act, 1884

CHAPTER XLV.

An Act to amend the National School Teachers (Ireland) Act, 1879, in so far as it relates to the Loans for Teachers’ Residences. [7th August 1884.]

[*Preamble*.]

### 1. Short title.

**1.** This Act may be cited for all purposes as the National School Teachers Amendment (Ireland) Act, 1884.

### 2. Extension of power to make loans for residences.

**2.** [*Repeal of so much of 42 & 43 Vict. c. 74 as limits the operation of that Act to certain vested schools*.] All schools, whether non-vested or vested, and if vested irrespectively of the date of vesting, shall be entitled to the benefit of loans for teachers residences.
