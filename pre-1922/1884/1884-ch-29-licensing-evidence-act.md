# Licensing (Evidence) Act, 1884

CHAPTER XXIX.

An Act to extend Section forty-one of the Licensing Act, 1872. [28th July 1884.]

[*Preamble recites* 35 *&* 36 *Vict. c*. 94*. s*. 41.]

### 1. Extension of 35 & 36 Vict. c. 94. s. 41.

**1.** Section forty-one of the Licensing Act, 1872, shall be construed as if after the words “application is for the grant of a license” there were inserted the words “or for the transfer of a license.”

Provided that the magistrates or justices shall be satisfied by evidence submitted to them that the license was withheld without any legal right to withhold the same.

### 2. Short title.

**2.** This Act may be cited as the Licensing (Evidence) Act, 1884.
