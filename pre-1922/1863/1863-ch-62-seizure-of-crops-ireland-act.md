# Seizure of Crops (Ireland) Act, 1863

CHAPTER LXII.

An Act to amend the Law relating to the Seizure of growing Crops in Ireland. [21st July 1863.]

[*Preamble.*]

### 1. Extent of Act.

**1.** This Act shall extend to Ireland only.

### 2. Growing crops, &c. not to be seized under civil bill decrees or justices orders.

**2.** It shall not be lawful to seize or take in execution under any civil bill decree or order of the civil bill court, or under any decree, order, or warrant of a justice of the peace, any growing crops, trees, shrubs, plants, or vegetable matters which are not severed from the land and soil, …
