# Carriers Act Amendment Act, 1865

CHAPTER XCIV.

An Act to amend the Carriers Act. [5th July 1865.]

### 1. Meaning of “lace” in 11 Geo. 4. and 1 Will. 4. c. 68.

**1.** In the Carriers Act, 1830, the term “lace” shall, with respect to any parcel or package delivered after the commencement of this Act, be construed as not including machine-made lace.

[*S*. 2 *rep*. 56 *&* 57 *Vict. c*. 14. (*S.L.R*.)]

### 3. Short title.

**3.** This Act may be cited as “The Carriers Act Amendment Act, 1865.”
