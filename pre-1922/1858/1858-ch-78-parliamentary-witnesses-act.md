# Parliamentary Witnesses Act, 1858

CHAPTER LXXVIII.

An Act to enable the Committees of both Houses of Parliament to administer Oaths to Witnesses in certain Cases. [2d August 1858.]

[*Preamble*.]

[*S*. 1 *rep*. 34 *&* 35 *Vict; c*. 83. *s*. 2.]

### 2. Committees of House of Lords may administer oaths.

**2.** Any committee of the House of Lords may administer an oath to the witnesses examined before such committee.

### 3. False evidence perjury.

**3.** Any person examined as aforesaid who shall wilfully give false evidence shall be liable to the penalties of perjury.
