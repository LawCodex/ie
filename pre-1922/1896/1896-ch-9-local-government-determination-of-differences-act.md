# Local Government (Determination of Differences) Act, 1896

CHAPTER IX.

An Act to amend certain Provisions of the Local Government Act, 1888, with respect to the Determination of Differences by the Local Government Board. [21st May 1896.]

[*S*. 1 *of this Act substitutes words in* 51 *&* 52 *Vict. c*. 41, *ss*. 11 (3), (4), (9), 63; *see that Act. S*. 2 *rep*. 8 *Edw*. 7. *c*. 49 (*S.L.R*.). *S*. 3 *conferred a Short Title on the Act*.]
