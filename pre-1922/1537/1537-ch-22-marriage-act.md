# Marriage Act 1537

c.22 Thact for marieng with Irishmen<sup>94</sup>

Forasmoche as by mariage alterage and fostering of the kinges subjectes of this his lande of Irlande with his graces Irishe rebels great lacke of obedience hathe growen to his highnes and his moost noble progenitours of their people within this lande and by the same dyverse enormyties myschieves and inconvenyances have ensued and comen to his said loving subjectes from tyme to tyme for the more parte during thiese CC yeres as evidentlie to every of the kinges subjcctes of this his lande for the tyme beyng having knowledge or practyse playnlie ded and dothe appere and though in the tyme of the kynges moost noble progenitours the same myschiefes and inconvenyences whiche followed sethens to the utter undoyng and desolacion of his graces said subjectes was then right wele perceived and dyverse good estatutes and actes by auctoritie of parliament therfor provyded wherin is conteyned that every of the kinges subjectes offendyng the same shulde be a traditor attaynted yet notwithstonding what by the defaulte and necligence of the heddes and rulers of this lande undre the kinges highnes that the said estatutes or actes were nat duclie put in execucion as the wilfull apperyt of his said subjectes havyng noo remembraunce of their bounden dueties to their moost drad souveraigne lorde their awne commoditie quietnes and profict ne the utilitie and publicque weale of their native cuntrey no yet the contempt or daungier of the transgressyng of the said actes ded often and many tymes aswele mary as fostre with the said Irishe rebelles and some tymes by fraude and covyne havyng then a perfect knowledge and an entent that every suche Irishe rebell with whom they entended to marie or foster wold in noo parte use hym selfe as to the kinges true and obedient subject shulde apperteigne but for their awne saulfgarde and discharge from the penaltie and daungier of the said estatutes or actes onelie to delude or defraude thordynaunce and purvey of the same ded procure to make the said Irishe rebelles with whom they entended soo to mary or foster denyzyns by the kinges moost gracious lettres patentes wher as the said Irishe rebels soo made denizins ded or doo in noo parte use theme selfe as to the kinges subjectes doo apperteyn through whiche the kinges highnes hathe lost for the moor parte thobediencie of his people of this his lande and diverse myschieves and inconvenyences to his said subjectes from tyme to tyme hathe ensued and moche more like to ensue onles that some good remedie or provision be had or provided in that behalfe; for reformacion wherof be it enacted ordeyned and establisshed by thauctoritic of this present parliament that noo persone ne persones the kinges subjectes within this his lande of what estate degree dignitie or condicion so ever he or they be shall marie or foster them selves their childer or kynsfolke within the fourthe degree or any of theme to or with any Irishe persone or persons of Irishe blood which be not the kinges true subjectes ne use theme selves accordynglie thogh any suche person or persones be made denizyns oneles that every suche persone soo to be made denizin doo his homage and feaultic befor the kynges chauncellor or keaper of the great scale for the tyme beyng to the kinges highnes swering thothe comprised in thact of succession for the fulfilling and accomplishement of theffect tenor and purport of the same and alsoo shalbe bound by recognisaunce befor the kinges chaunceller or keaper of his graces great seale of this his land for the tyme being in suche somes of monye as to the said chaunceller or keaper of the great seale shalbe thought meat and convenyent that he from the tyme that he is made denizin shalbe faithfull true and obedient to the kinges highnes his heyrs and successors and that everie persone and persones the kinges subjectes within this his lande that shall soo marie or foster theme selves their childer or kynsfolke or any of theme to or with any suche persone or persons of Irishe blood as is aforesaid without fraude or covine at the tyme of the said mariage or fostring shall have a true and unfayned intent and meanyng that the partie soo made denyzyn with whom he or they intend so to marie or foster will during his naturall life be faithfull true and obedient to the kinges highnes his heyrs and successors and if any persone or persons nowe made or whiche herafter shalbe made denizin at any tyme or tymes herafter with whom any suche mariage or fostring shalbe had or made doo transgresse his fidelitie and faithe to the kinges highnes his heyres or successors using hym silf as the kinges rebell and proclamacion therof made by the kinges lieutenaunt or deputie or by his or their commaundement by thadvyse of the kinges consaill in the shire or oppen markettes next adyoynyng to suche offendor then every suche of the kinges subjectes whiche soo shall have fostred or maried with any suche denyzyn soo rebelling or offending contrarie his othe or duetie shall utterlie avoide any wilfull famyliaritie or company with hym or theme that soo shall offende oncles it be to reconcile suche offendor or offendors to knowledge their offences commytted to the kinges majestic or els thentent to have restitucion of suche goodes as any suche persone persones soo offending have taken from any the kinges subjectes; and if any of his highnes subjectes of this his lande doo offende in die premysses or in any parcell therof that then every such offence shalbe demed highe treason and that every persone and persones soo offending being therof laufullic convicted according to the due ordre and proces of the kinges lawes shalbe adjudged a traditor attaynted of highe treason and shall have and suffre suche paynes of deathe losses and forfaictur of landes tenementes goodes and cattails as in cases of highe treason; and be it alsoo enacted by auctorite aforesaid that if any suche persone or persons made or to be made denizins doo not to his and their wit knowledge and power fulfill and accomplishe the teanor purport and effecte of the said othe and of the condicion of the said recognysaunce but wilfullic transgresse and withdraw their obedience and bounden duetie to their moste drad souveraigne lorde from his highnes that then if any suche persone or persones the kinges true subjectes their freendes or servauntes that hathe maried or fostred or at any tyme hereafter shall marie or foster them selves their childer or kynsfolkes or eny of theme to or with any suche presone or persons made or to be made denyzins will or do aide maynteyne supporte succour counseill favour or by eny other meane encourage any suche persone or persons soo made or to be made denizins contemptuouselie offending as is aforsaide oneles it be to reconcile and bryng theme to their duetie of obedience due to their moost drad souveraigne lorde or els to have restitucion of goods by theme taken as is aforsaid that then he or they soo offending and every of theme being therof laufully convicted according the due ordre and proces of the kinges lawes shalbe adjudged a traditor attaynted of highe treason and shall have and suffre suche paynes of deathe losses and forfaictur of landes tenementes goods and cattails as in cases of high treason; and be it further enacted that all and every article clause and sentence comprised in any other acte or actes which be repugnaunt or contraric to theffect tenor and purport of this acte or any thing therin conteyned shalbe voide to all ententes and purposes; provided that this acte be not in force or take execucion befor the same be proclamed in every shire in due forme.
