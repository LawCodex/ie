# Inland Navigation Act 1537

c.36 Thact for the werres upon the Barrowe and other waters in the countie of Kilkenny<sup>158</sup>

Prayen the comens in this present parliament assembled that wher at all tymes necessarie bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels loden and bestowed with goodes merchaundises and other stuffe have bene used to passe and repasse through and in the kynges moost excellent majesties ryvers and waters of the Barowe the Noyre the Suyr and the Rie within this lande whiche Rye is in the countie of Kylkenny to and from the kynges citie of Waterforde and the townes of Kilkenny Rosse and Clomell to and from dyvers borrowe and corporate townes and other places beyng situated in the counties of Kildare Catherlaugh Wexforde Kilkenny Waterforde and Tipperary throughe whiche great commoditie and profict haithe growen and myght growe to the saide citie townes brughes and other places and to all and every the kinges true subjectes adjoynyng to the same waters and ryvers; and yet this notwithstonding nowe of late dyverse wilfull persones havyng no respecte to the premysses but more rather to their awne wilfulnes synguler commoditie and benefict havyng noo grounde ne cause reasonable to the same have in dyverse places of the saide ryvers and waters made souche werres purpresturs ingynnes streites and other like obstacles and theme and every of theme soo do kepe and maynteyne that by noo meanes any bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels laden and bestowed as is aforsaide can convenyentlie passe and repasse and through whiche the salmon frie bredyng in the saide waters and ryvers be clerelie destroied contrarie to theffect and purporte of the statutes therin provided and in divers sondrie places of the saide ryvers and waters alsoo doo take and procure to be taken dyvers exaccions and unlaufull custumes callid bankfees of any bote scowte whurrie claran coet and other vessel laden and bestowed as is aforsaide goyng and passyng in and through the kynges saide ryvers and waters albeit that parte of the saide borrowe townes by their liberties and chartors be frelie and quyte discharged of all maner tolles passages custumes and pondages in the saide ryvers and waters and the lande on every side of the same and that alsoo souche persone and persons as be the botemen and have the rule governaunce and conveyeng of the saide bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels laden and bestowed as is aforsaide at souche tymes as they be laded with wynes of their deceipt and craftic meanes doo from tyme to tyme drawe drynke and take with theme parcell of the saide wynes and some tymes puttyng in the vessell where the saide wyne was to replenyshe the same the quantite or somoche water wherby the residue of the saide wyne is clerelie destroyed to the great losses and damages of the veray owners therof throughe whiche premisses the merchauntes occupiers and dwellers of the saide citie townes and burghes and places be soo discoraged and indamaged that they be more liker to give over and withdraw theme selves from souche tradde of merchaundises then any further to use or excercise the same whiche is not onelie thympoverysshing of the saide merchauntes occupiers and dwellers but alsoo the utter decay of the saide borrowe townes and places oneles that some convenyant remedie therin be provided; wherfor be it enacted ordeyned and established by auctoritic of this present parliament that it shalbe laufull and justifiable to and for every of<sup>159</sup> the kynges subjectes within this lande goyng in company with any sherif or senescall of any countie afor named to prostrate and brecke and cause to be prostrated and broken from tyme to tyme all and every souche werres purpresturs ingynnes streytes and other like obstacles made or hereafter to be made in any of the said ryvers or waters and also a convenyent gappe or place where that bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels may passe and repasse in of upon and throwe every milponde made or hereafter to be made in [*m 42*] any of the said ryvers or waters; and if any persone or persones doo resiste or make any defence in or for the prostratyng and breckyng or causyng to be prostrated and brocken of the saide werres purpresturs ingynnes streites and other like obstacles and milpondes in maner and forme as is aforesaide that then every souche persone and persones soo makyng resistence or defence for every souche tyme shall forfaict v<sup>li.</sup> thoon, moitie therof to the kinges highnes his heyres and successors and thother moitie therof to the partie that woll sue for the same in any the kynges courtes by acccion of debt bill playnt enformacion or otherwise wherin noo essoyne proteccion ne wageor of lawe shalbe admytted or allowed; and further be it enacted by auctoritie aforesaide that the sherif of any countie and the senescall of eny libertie for the tyme beyng wherin any souche werres purpresturs ingynnes streytes and other like obstacles in any of the saide ryvers and waters is made or hereafter shalbe made or wher ther is or shalbe any milponde wherof ther is nor shalbe noo gappe as is aforesaide after knowledge therof had by reaporte compaynt enquvrie or otherwise shall from tyme to tyme callyng and takyng with hym if he he sherif the power of the countie and if he be senescall the power of the libertie goo in proper persone to every souche place wheras eny werre purpresture ingynne streyte or other like obstacle in any of the saide ryvers and waters is or shalbe made or wher ther is or shalbe any milponde wherof ther is or shalbe eny gappe brocken as is aforsaide and the same prostrate and brecke or cause to be prostrated and brocken in maner and forme as is aforsaide upon payne to every souche shirif and senescall as often as he or they shall soo offende or refuse to prostrate and brecke or cause to be prostrated and brocken any of the saide werres purpresturs ingynnes streytes and other like obstacles and a gappe in any souche milponde of x<sup>li.</sup> thone halfe to the kynges highnes his heyres and successors and thother halfe to the partie that woll sue for the same in eny of the kynges courtes by accion of debt bill pleynt enformacion or otherwise wherin noo essoyne proteccion ne wageor of lawe shalbe admytted or allowed; and if eny persone or persones dwellyng or demurraunt within any souche countie doo refuse and woll not goo in aide of the shirif of the same countie and if eny persone or persones dwelling or demurraunt within any souche libertie doo refuse and woll not goo in aide of the senescall of the same for the prostratyng and breckyng or causing to be prostrated and brocken of any the saide milpondes werres ingynnes purpresturs streytes and other like obstacles made or to be made in any of the saide ryvers or waters in maner and forme as is aforsaide beyng laufullie summoned and warned for the same for every souche tyme shall forfaict X<sup>s.</sup> thoon halfe therof to the kyng his heyres and successors and thother halfe to eny souche<sup>160</sup> shirif or senescall that woll sue for the same in any the kynges courtes by accion of debt bill pleynte enformacion or otherwis wherin noo essoyne proteccion ne wageor of lawe shalbe admytted or allowed; be it further enacted by auctoritie aforsaide that noo persone ne persons bodies politicque ne corporate within this lande by hym or theme selves his or their constables fermors officers or servauntes shall take Icvie distrayne or receive or suffre permyt or cause to be taken levied distrayned or received noo custumes or for any custumes called banefees ne any other duetie rent or custome of any bore scowte whurrie claran coet or any other vessell what soo ever it be passyng or repasssyng in or through any the saide ryvers or waters or any parcell of theme or of any boote or other vessell wherin any persone or persones doo fyshe in any of the saide ryvers or waters soo fur as the salte water dothe ebbe and flowe in any of the saide ryvers or of thowners of any goodes merchaundises victuall or stuffe then beyng in any souche bote scowte whurrie clarane or coet and other vessels as is aforsaide or of any the marryners botemen and other rulers and conveyors of the saide<sup>161</sup> bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels but that they and every of theme shall peasiblic suffre and permyt the saide botes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels as is afor specified with all the goodes merchaundises victuall fyshe and stuffe comprised and beyng in the same and thawners of the saide goodes mercchaundises victuals fishe and other stuffe and their servauntes and the maryners bootmen and other rulers and conveyers of the saide bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels in uppon and through every the saide ryvers and every parcell of theme and the lande adjoynyng to the same and that the saide awners their servauntes maryners botemen and other rulers and conveyers and all other persones comyng in aide and helpe of them and every of theme at all souche tymes as the same maryners botemen and other rulers and conveyers shall thinke the same necessarie and nedefull shall have and occupie at every of their willes and pleasurs the space and bread of vij sote or more as neade requyre of playne grounde upon every parte of the lande of every side of every the saide ryvers and waters next adjoynyng to the said ryvers and waters and that to be where they must nedes drawe the saide botes and other vessels afor named with streinght of horses or men by lande oneles that at the making of this present acte ther be a castle fortresse orchade or gardyng upon any souche lande soo adjoynyng to the saide ryvers of every side of the water soo fur and as longe as the saide ryvers or waters doo ronne or have their concource for the drawyng takyng lodyng and conveyeng of the saide bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels and every of theme without any impedyment let vexacion disturbaunce or inquietnes of any the possessioners or enheritors of the saide landes or any parte of theme or any persone or persones bodies politicque or corporate offending in to every persone and persones bodies polcticque and corporate offendyng in the premisses or any parcell therof as often as he or they shall soo offende treble the valor of that thing that they or any of theme doo take to be forfaiet and yeven to the partie greved and over that x<sup>li.</sup> thone halfe therof to the kyng his heyres successors and assignes and thother halfe to the partie that woll sue for the same in any the kynges courtes by accion of debt bill pleynt enformacion or otherwise wherin noo essoyne proteccion ne wageor of lawe shalbe admytted or allowed; and alsoo considring the trust and confidence that thowners of any wynes doo put in the saide maryners botemen rulers and conveyers of the saide botes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels what soo ever they be for the true conveyaunce and custodie of the saide wynes the deceipt and craftie handlyng of the saide maryners botemen rulers and conveyers of the saide botes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels and the losses and damages that haithe and may hereafter ensue to the owners of any souche wynes be it enacted by auctoritic aforsaide that if any maryner bootman ruler or conveyer of any the saide bootes scowtes whurries clarans coettes and other vessels have the carryeng freight or conveyeng of any wynes goodes or other merchaundises in any bote scowte whurrie claran coet or other vessel in or throughe the saide ryvers or waters or any parcell of theme or if any persone or persones take upon hym or theme the carrieng or conveyeng of eny wyne goodes or other merchaundises by lande in carte wayne or otherwise [*m.43*] and doo drawe drynke take or wilfullie perishe any caske or parcell of eny souche wynes goodes or merchaundises to theme or any of theme to be delivered at any tyme hereafter to be conveyed carried or transportid in the said ryvers or waters or in any parcell of theme or to any place or quarter of this domynyon by lande in maner and forme as is aforsaide shall forfaiet and lose to the owner or owners of the saide wynes goodes or merchaundises as often as he or they shall offende the same asmoche money as he or they shoulde have for the freight conveyeng and cariage of all the wynes goodes and merchaundises to hym or theme at that tyme delyvered or appoynted to be conveyed or carried either by any of the side ryvers or waters or to any other place by lande ane the treble valor of that thing or thinges that he or they doo take drawe drynke or wilfullie perishe or cause to be taken drawen dronken or wilfullie perished of the saide wynes goodes or merchaundises and over that to forfaiet v<sup>li.</sup> thone moitie therof to oure souveraigne lorde the kyng his heyres and successors and thother moitie to the partie that woll sue for the same in any the kynges courtes by accion of debt bill pleynt enformacion or otherwise wherin noo essoyne protection ne wageor of lawe shalbe admytted or allowed; and further be it enacted by auctoritie aforsaide that none of the saide maryners or botemen or other persones afornamed shall take or receive or cause to be taken or received any somes of monye or any other thyng for the freight cariage or conveyng of any stuffe or merchaundises what soo ever they be other then souche like as is comprised in thextent therof made remaynyng in the custodie of rhe sherif of the countie of Tipperarie and in other places wher souche somes as they shoulde have is not mencioned or lymyted in the saide extente that then they and every of theme do take noo more wages ne somes of monye but souche like somes of monye and wages as they and every of theme in tyme passed have bene<sup>162</sup> moost comenlie used and accustumed to take or receive for the freight conveyeng transportyng and carriage of eny souch<sup>163</sup> stuffe goodes or merchaundises uppon payne to the taker as often as he shall offende in the same xiij<sup>s.</sup> iiij<sup>d.</sup> thone halfe therof to our souveraigne lorde the king his heyres and successors and thother halfe to the partie that woll sue for the same in any of the kynges courtes or befor the kinges commissioners by accion of debt bill pleynt enformacion or otherwise wherin noo essoyne proteccion ne wageor of lawe shalbe admytted or allowed.
