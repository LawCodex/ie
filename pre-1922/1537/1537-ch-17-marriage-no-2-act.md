# Marriage (No. 2) Act 1537

**c.17 Thacte of succession betwixt the kyng and qucane Jane**

In theire moost humble wise showeth unto your moost roiall majestic the lordes spirituall and temporall and all other your moost loving and obedient subjectes the comens in this your highe courte of parliament assembled that when in your last parliament begonne and holden at Dublin the furst daie of Maij in the xxviij<sup>ti</sup> yere of your moost gracious reigne<sup>74</sup> it was made and enordynied an acte concernyng amonges many thinges aswele for ratificacion and confirmacion of your mariage then had and solempnysed betwene your highnes and the late lady Anne Boleyn marques of Pembroke as for the lymytacion of the succession of your dominyon and lordship of this lande of Irland to thissue of your bodie begotten and to be begotten of the bodie of the said lady Anne with dyverse remaynders over for defaulte of suche yssue as in the said acte pleynlie and particulerlie more at large is expressed and mencioned and wheralsoo in by the said acte it was ordeyned and establisshed that all and singuler your subjectes shuld take a corporall othe for the fulfilling maynteynyng and defendyng thoole effectes and<sup>75</sup> contentes of the saide acte and it was further ordeyned by the said acte amonges other thinges that if eny persone or persones subject or resiaunt within the dominion of this londe after the saide furst daie of Maij by writing print dede or acte procured ded or cause to be procured or doon eny thing or thinges to the prejudice sclaundre disturbaunce or derogacion of the said matrimonie solempnysed betwene your majestie and the said lady Anne or to the perill sclaundre or disherison of eny thissue and heyres of your highnes being lymyted by the same acte to enherit and to be enheritable to the said dominion and lordship of this lond wherby any suche yssues or heyres of your highnes myght be distroyed disturbed or interrupted in bodie or title of enheritaunce to the saide dominion and lordshipp of this londe as to theme is lymyted in the same acte that then every suche persone and persons their aidors councellours mayntenors and abettors and every of theme for every suche offence should be adjudged highe traditors and that everie suche offence should be adjudged highe treason and thoffendors and their aidors councellours mayntenors and abettors and every of theme being lawfullie convieted of suche offence by presentment veredict confession or proces according to the costume and lawes of the realme of Englond shulde suffre paynes of deathe as in cases of highe treason as in the said acte amonges many other articules clauses and provisions is more at large mencioned and specified; and albeit moost draid souveraigne lordc that the said acte were then made as it was then thought by your majestie nobles and comens upon a pure perfict and clere foundacion thinking the said mariage then had betwene your highnes and the said lady Anne in their conscience to have bene pure sincere perfect and good and soo was reputed accepted and taken in the realme of England and this land of Irland til nowe of late that God of his infynit goodnes from whom noo secrete thinges can be hidde hathe caused to be broght to light cvydent and oppen knowledge aswele certein just true and laufull impedymentes unknowen at the making of the said acte and sithen that tyme confessed by the saide lady Anne befor the moost reverend father in God Thomas archebusshop of Canterburie metropolitan and primat of all Englonde sitting judicially for the same by the whiche playnelie appereth that the said mariage betwene your grace and the saide lady Anne was never good nor consonaunt to the lawes but utterlie voide and of noon effecte by reason wherof your highnes was and is laufullie deforced and separated from the bandes of the said mariage in the life of the seid ladie Anne; and this notwithstanding moost gracious souveraigne lorde the ladie Elizabeth your doughter being borne undre the saide unlaufull mariage by vertuc and aucthorite of thact of your succession above remembred for lacke of heyre males of your bodie should immediatelie succede as your laufull heyre in the moste hie estate of your dominion and lordship of this lond of Irlond against all honour equitie reason and good conscience if remedie shulde not be provyded for the same and over this moost draid souveraigne lorde albeit that your majestie not knowing of eny laufull impedimentes entred into the bandes of the saide unlaufull mariage and advaunced the same lady Anne to the honour of the souverain estate of quene of this realme of Englond yet nervertheless she enflammed with pride and carnall desires of her boddy putting aparte the dread of God and excellent benefictes received of your highnes confeterated hersilf with George Bulleyn late lord Rochefourth her naturall brother Henry Norres esquyer Fraunces Weston knyght William Bruarton esquier gentlemen of your privaie chambre and Marke Smeton grome of your saide privaie chambre and soo being confederat she and they moost traditorslie commytted and perpetrated divers detestable and abhomynable treasons to the moost ferefull perill and daungier of the distruccion of your moost royall persone and to thutter losse disherison and desolacion of this lande yf God of his goodnes had not in due tyme brought the said treasons to light for the whiche being pleynlie and manyfestly proved they were convicted and attaynted by the due course and ordre of your comen laws of the saide realme ot Englond and hath suffred according to their demerites as by the recordes therof more at large may appere; and albeit moost gracious and moost draid souveraigne lorde dyverse and sondry honourable just true and reasonable clauses be conteyned and expressed in the said act made for thestablishement of your succession whiche be veray necessary and profictable for the comen wealthe of this your londe yet nevertheles bicause certein articles and clauses conteyned in the same concernyng the ratificacion of your said unlaufull mariage betwene your highnes and the said lady Anne and the limitacion of your succession to the yssues of your body had by the said lady Anne and thothe expressed and mencioned in the saide acte above remembred for the defendyng and maynteynyng thoole effectes and contentes of the saide acte which clauses and articles be nowe become of late soo dishonourable and soo farre distaunt from the due course of your comen lawes of your realme of Englande and this your lande and alsoo soo moche agaynst good reason equitie and good conscience that they can not be susteyned ne tollerated to contynue and endure without great perill of division hereafter to be had by occasion of the same amonges us your saide moost humble loving and obedient subjectes and our posterities it may therfor please your highnes of your moost excellent and accustumable goodnes and for thentier love favour and herty affeccion that your majestie hathe alwayes borne and beareth to the comen wealthe of this your lond and for the conservacion of the good peaux unytie and rest of us your moost bounden and obedyent subjectes and of our said posterities that it may be enacted by aucthoritic of this present parliament that the said acte and all clauses articles and provisions therin conteyned from the said furst day of May shalbe repelled adnulled and made frustrate and of none effecte; provided alwey that the saide repeale and adnullacion of the said acte whiche was made for thestablishement of your succession shall not be taken expounded nor understonde to discharge any persone or persons whiche have done or commytted eny offences of treasone or mesprision of treason contrarie to the same acte or eny parte therof at eny tyme afor the said furst daie of Maij but that suche offenses of treason and mesprision of treason doone and commytted by eny persone or persons contrary to the same acte afor the said furst day of Maij shalbe and remayne in the same degree and qualitie to be punyshed by aucthoritie of the same acte as if the same acte had not be repealed the repeale and adnullacion of the same acte by aucthoritie of this present parliament in eny wise notwithstonding; and that it be further enacted by aucthoritie aforsaid that the said late quene Anne, George Bulleyn late lorde Rochefourth Henry Norres esquyer Fraunces Weston knyght William Bruarton esquyer and Marke Smeton for their abhomynable and detestable treasons by theme and every of theme moost abhominably and traditorsly commytted and doon against your majestie your realme of Englond and this your lande of Irland shalbe by aucthoritie of this present parliament convicted and attaynted of highe treason in suche maner and forme and accordingly as they by the due course and ordre of your lawes of your saide realme and that the said late queue Anne George Bulleyn late lorde Rochfourth Henry Norres esquyer Fraunees Weston knyght William Bruarton esquyer and Marke Smeton and every of theme shall lose and forfeict to your highnes and to your heyres all suche their manors meases londes tenementes rentes revercions remayndors uses possessions offices rightes condicions and all other hereditamentes of what names nature or qualities soo ever they be whiche they or eny of theme or eny other to their use or to the use of eny of theme had or ought to<sup>76</sup> have had of eny state of enheritaunce in fee symple or in taile in use or in possession at the dayes of their severall treasons commytted lymyted and expressed in their indictment or at eny tyme after saving to every persone and persones and bodies poleticque and to theyres assignes and successors of every of theme other then the said late quene and thother traditors afor named and their heyres and assignes and theyres assignes and successors of every of theme and all and every other persone and persones claymyng by theme or any of theme or to their uses or to the use of any of theme or to thuse of any of their heyres all suche right title use possession interest condicion fees offices rentes annuyties comens and all other commodities hereditamentes what sooever they or any of theme myght shuld or ought to have had if this acte had never bene had ne made; and over this moost gracious souveraigne lorde forasmoche as it hathe pleasid your moost royall majestie notwithstonding the great and intollerable perils and occasions whiche your highnes hathe suffred and susteyned aswele by occasion of your furst unlaufull mariage solempnysed betwene your highnes and the lady Katheryn late prynces dowager as by occasion of the said unlaufull mariage betwene your highnes and the late quene Anne at the moost humble peticion and intercession of your nobles of your said realme of Englond for thardent love and fervent affeccion whiche your highnes bearith to the conservacion of the peaux and unytie of this same realme and of this your lande of Irland and for the good and quiet gouvernaunce therof of your moost excellent goodness to entre into mariage ageyne and have chosen and taken a right noble vertuouse and excellent lady quene Jane to your true and laufull wife and have laufully celebrated and solempnysed mariage with her according to the lawes of holy churche who fo[r] her convenyent yeres excellent beawtie and purenes of fleshe and blood is apte God willing to conceive yssue by your highnes whiche mariage is soo pure and syncere without spot doubte or ympedyment that thyssue procreated under the same (wh[en] it shall please almyghty God to send it) cannot be laufully truclie nor justely interrupted or disturbed of the right and title [and] succession of your dominyon of this lande; it may therfor nowe please your moost gracious majestie at the moost humble peticion and intercession of us your nobles and comens assembled in this present parliament aswele for the clere extynguyshement of all [*m. 16*] ambiguities and doubtes as for a pure a<sup>77</sup> perficte unitie of us your moost humble and obedient subjectes and of all our posterities that it be enacted by your moost gracious and royall majestie by consent of us the lordes spirituall and temporall and the comens in this present parliament assembled and by auctoritie of the same in manner and forme as hereafter in articles ensucth; furst forasmoche as the marriage heretofor solempnysed betwene your highnes and the saide lady Katheryn late princes dowager decessed which afor was laufull wife to your naturall brother prynce Arthure and by hym carnally knowen as hathe ducly bene proved by sufficient witnes at all tymes was is and ought to be dcamed and determyned to be agaynst the lawes of almyghty God and not dispensable by eny humane aucthoritie and soo hathe bene deamed and determined aswele by thoole clergy of this realme in bothe convocacions and by bothe thunyversities therof and by thunyversities of Bonanye Padwey Parys Orleans Tullus Angeo and dyverse other and alsoo by a great nombre of privat writynges of many right excellent wele lerned men; be it therfor enacted by auctoritie of this present parliament according as it was ordeyned and enacted in the said acte made in the last parliament for thestablishement of your succession that the said mariage heretofor had and solempnysed betwene your highnes and the saide lady Katheryn whiche was befor laufull wife to the saide prynce Arthur your elder brother and by hym carnally knowen as dothe duclic appere by sufficient prove in a laufull proces had and made befor the said moost reverend father in God Thomas by the suffraunce of God archebusshop of Canterbury metropolitane and prymat of all Englond shalbe by auctoritie of this present parliament dyffynytyvelie clerely and absolutely declared demed and adjudged to be agaynst the lawes of almyghty God and alsoo accepted reputed and taken of noo value ne effecte but utterly voide and adnychillat and that the said separacion therof made by the said archebusshop shalbe good and effectuall to all ententes and purposes any licence dispensacion or eny other acte or actes goyng before or ensuyng the same or to the contrarie thereof in eny wise notwithstonding and that every suche lycence dispensacion acte or actes thing or thinges had made done or to be doon to the contrarie thereof shalbe to all ententes void and of noon effect and soo shalbe reputed and taken by and amonges all us your true subjectes and inhabitantes of and in this your lande and dominion of Irland for ever and that thyssue borne and procreated undre the same unlaufull mariage made and solempnysed betwene your highnes and the said lady Katheryn shalbe taken demed and accepted illegittimat to all ententes and purposes and shalbe utterly forclosed excluded and bard to clayme chalendge or demaunde eny enheritaunces as laufull heyre to your highnes by lynyall discent; and also forasmoche as the mariage solempnysed betwen your highnes and the saide late quene Anne uppon true and just causes was and is demed and adjudged by the said moost reverend father to be of noo value no effecte and a devorce and separacion therof had and made by the due ordre and proces of the lawes of the churche of Englond befor the said reverend father be it therfor enacted by auctoritie of this present parliament that the same mariage betwene your highnes and the said late quene Anne shalbe taken reputed demed and adjudged to be of noo force streingth vertue nor effect and that the separacion and devorce therof had by the sentence and myndement of the said moost reverend father in God shalbe good and effectuall and soo hereafter shalbe taken and reputed for ever by all your subjectes their heyres and successors and that noon appele reapele revocacion or adnullacion therof nor eny parte of the same shall hereafter be had taken allowed or admytted in eny behalfe and that all thyssues and children borne and procreat under the same mariage betwene your highnes and the said late queue Anne shalbe taken reputed and accepted to be illegittimat to all ententes and purposes and utterlie forclosed excluded and bard to clayme chalenge or demaund eny enheritaunce as laufull heyre or heyres to your highnes by lynyall discent the saide formor acte made in the last parliament for thestablishement of your succession or eny thing therin conteyned or eny other thing or thinges to the contrarie thereof notwithstonding; and ferthermore sythens many inconveyences have fallen aswele within this lande as in the others by reason of marieng within the degrees of mariage prohibited by Goddes lawes that is to saye the son to mary the mother or the stepmother carnally knowen by his father the brother the sustre the father his sonnes doghter or his doghters doghter or the sonne to mary the doghter of his father procreated and borne by his stepmother or the son to mary his awnte being his father or mothers suster or to mary his uncles wyfe carnally knowen by his uncle or the father to mary his sonnes wife carnally knowen by his son or the brother to mary his brothers wife carnally knowen by his brother or eny man maried and carnally knowing his wife to mary his wifes doghter or his wifes sonnes doghter or his wifes doghters doghter or his wife sustre, and further to delate and declare the meanyng of thiese prohibicions it is to be undrestoud that if it chaunce any man to knowe carnally eny woman that then all and synguler persons being in any degree of consanguynytie or affynytie as is above written to eny of the parties so carnally offending shalbe demed and adjudged to be within the cases and lymytes of the seid prohibicions of mariage all whiche mariages albeit they be pleynly prohibited and detested by the lawes of God yet nevertheles at some tymes they have preceded under colours of dispensacions by mans power whiche is but usurped and of right ought not to be granted admytted ne allowed for noo man of what estate degree or condicion soo ever he be hathe power to dispence with Goddes lawes as all the clergie of the realme of Englond in the said convocacions and the moost parte of all the unyversities of Cristendome and we alsoo doo affyrme and thinke; be it therfor enacted by auctoritie aforsaid according as is declared and conteyned in the said acte made in the last parliament for thestablishment of your succession that noo persone or persons subjectes or resiauntes of this lond of what estate degree or dignytie soo ever thei be shall from hensforth mary within the degrees afor rehersed what pretence soo ever shalbe made to the contrarie therof and in case eny persone or persons of what estat dignytie degree or condicion soo ever they be hathe bene heretofor maried within the realme of Englonde or this londe within eny degrees above rehersed and by eny thearchebusshops or mynystres of the churche of Englond or<sup>78</sup> of Irland be separat from the bandes of suche unlaufull mariages that then every suche separacion shalbe good laufull firme and permanent for ever and not by eny power aucthoritie or means to be revoked or undoon here-after and that the children proceding or procreat under suche unlaufull mariage shall not be laufull ne legittimat eny foreyn lawes licences dispensacions or other thing or thinges to the contrarie hereof notwithstondyng and that in case ther be eny person or persons within this dominion and land alredy maryed within eny of the said degrees above specified and not yet seperated from the bondes of the said unlaufull mariage that then every suche persone soo unlaufully maried shalbe spereated by the diffinitive sentence and judgement of the said archebusshops busshops and other mynystres of the churche of<sup>79</sup> Englond or of Irland within the lymytes of their jurisdiccions and auctorities and by none other forreyn power or auctoritie and that all sentence and judgementes yeven and to be yeven by eny archbusshop busshop or other mynystre of the chruche of Englond or of Irland within the lymytes of their jurisdiccions and auctoritie shalbe diffynytive ferme good and effectuall to all ententes and be observed and obeid without sewyng any provocacions appels prohibicions or other proces from or to the court of Rome to the derogacion therof or contrarie to thact made sithen the begynnyng of the last parliament for restraint of suche provocacions apels prohibicions and other proces; and alsoo be it enacted by auctoritie aforsaid that all thyssue hereafter to be had and procreated betwene your highnes and your said moost dere and entierlie biloved laufull wife quene Jane shalbe your laufull children and heyres and be enheritable and enherit according to the course of enheritaunce and lawes of the realme of Englonde and this your lande of Irland the dominyon and lordship of the same lond with all dignyties honours preemynences prerogatives auctorities and jurisdiccions to the same annexed or belongyng in as ample and large maner as your highnes at this present tyme hath the same as king of the realme of Englond and lorde of this dominion and lande, rhenheritaunce therof to be and remayne to your said children and right heyres in maner and forme as hereafter shalbe declared, that is to saie furst the seid dominion and lordship of this lond and other the premisses shalbe to your majestie and to your heyres of your bodie laufully begotten knyt and unyted to thymperiall crowne of the realme of Englond that is to say to the furst son of your body betwene your highnes and your seid laufull wife quene Jane begotten and to theyres of the boddy of the seid furst son laufully begotten and for default of suche heyres then to the second son of your boddy and of the boddie of the said quene Jane begotten and to theyres of the body of the same second son laufully begotten as aforsaid and soo to every son of your bodie and of the body of the said quene Jane begotten and to theyres of the body of every suche son begotten according to the course of enheritaunce in that behalfe knyt and unyted to thimperiall crowne of the realme of Englond for ever and if it shall happen your said moost dere and intierly biloved wife quene Jane to deceasse without yssue male of the body of your highnes to be gotten (whiche God defend) then the same dominion and lordship and all other the premysses to be to your majestie as is aforsaid and to the son and heyre male of your body laufullie begotten by any other laufull wife and to the heyres of the body of the same son and heyre male laufully begotten and for default of suche yssue then to your second son of your body laufully begotten and to the heyres of the body of the same second son laufully begotten and soo from son and heire male to son and heyre male and to the heyres of the severall bodies of every suche son and heyre male laufully begotten according to the course of enheritaunce in like maner and forme as is above said, and for default of suche sonnes of your bodie laufully begotten and of the heyres of the severall bodies of every suche son laufully begotten that th[en] the said dominion and lordship and other the premisses shalbe to thyssue female betwene your majestie and your said moost dere and intier[ly] biloved wife quene Jane begotten and for lacke of suche yssue then to theyres femals of your bodie laufully begotten by any other laufull wife that is to saie furst to theldest yssue female and to the heyres of hir body laufully begotten and soo from yssue female to yssue female and to their heyres of their bodies one after an other by course of enheritaunce according to their ag[es] as the crowne of Englond hath bene accustumed and ought to succede and goo in case when ther is heyre female enheritable to the same; and forasmoche as it standeth at this present tyme in thoonlie will and pleasure of almyghtie God whether your majestie shall have heyres begotten and procreated betwene your highnes and your said moost dere entierlie beloved wife quene Jane or els any laufull heyres and yssues hereafter of your owne body begotten by any other lauful wife and if [suche] heyres shuld faile (as God defende) and non provysion made in your life who shuld rule and governe this lande for lacke of suche heyres that then this lande after your transitorie life shalbe destuted of a laufull governour or else percace encombred with suche a persone that would coveit to aspire to the same whom the subjectes of this lande shall not fynde in their hartes to love dred or obediently serve as their souveraigne lorde and if your grace (afor it may be certeinlie knowen whether ye shall have heyres or noo) shuld sodcynlic name and declare any persone or persones to succede after your deccasse for lacke of heyres of your body laufully begotten into the roiall estate of the dominyon of this lande then it is to be doubted that suche persone that shulde be soo named myght happen take great hart and courage and by presumpcion fall to inobedyence and rebellion by occasion of whiche premisses great division and dissencion may be and is verie likely to aryse and spring in this lande to the great perill and distruccion of us your moost humble and obedient subjectes and of all our posterities if remedie for the same should not be provyded; for reformacion and remedie wherof we your moost bounden and loving subjectes moost obedientlie knowledging that your majestie mooste [*m.17*] victoriouselie prudentlie poleticklie and indifferentlie haith maynteyned defended governed and rewled this your lande in good peauxe rest quietnes and obedyence during all the tyme of your moost graciouse reigne whiche we moost hertillie deasire myght contynwe for ever putting all our hole trust and confidence in your highnes and nothing doubting but that your majestie if ye shuld faile heires of your body laufully begotten whiche God defend, for the harty love and fervent affeccion that ye beare to this your dominion and lande and for avoiding all the thoccassions of devision afor rehersed so ernestlie myndeth the wealthe of the same that ye can best and moost prudently provyde suche a governour for us and this your land as shall and will succede and followe in the just and right tradde of all your procedinges and maynteyn kepe and defende the same and all the lawes and ordynaunces establisshed in your moost graciouse tyme for the wealth of this dominyon and lande whiche all we desire wherby we your said moost loving and obedient subjectes and our heires and successors shall and maie live as nere as may be in as good peaux unytie and obedience after your deceasse as we have lived in the tyme of your moost gracious reigne; doo therfor moost humble beseche your highnes that it may be enacted for avoidyng of ambiguities doubtes devysions and occasions in that behalf by your moost roiall majestie by thassent of us the lordes spirituall and temporall and the comens in this your present parliament assembled and by auctoritie of the same that your highnes shall have full and plenarie power and auctoritie to yeve dispose appoint assigne declare and lymyt by your lettres patentes undre your great seale or els by your last will made in writing and assigned with your moost gracious hande at your onelic pleasure from tyme to tyme hereafter your highe estat in and of the dominyon and lordship of this lond and all other the premisses therunto belongyng to be remayne succede and come after your deceasse and for lacke of laufull heyres of your body to be procreated and begotten as is afor lymyted by this acte to suche persone or persones in possession and remaindre as shall please your highnes and according to suche estate and after suche maner fourme facion ordre and condicion as shalbe expressed named declared and lymyted in your said lettres parentes or by your said last will: and we your moost humble and obedient subjectes doo faithfully promyse to your majestie by one comon assent that after your deccasse and for lacke of heyres of your body laufully begotten as is afor rehersed we our heyres and successors shall accepte and take love dred serve and alonely obey suche persone and persones males or females as your majestie shall give your said dominion of this lande unto by auctoritie of this said acte and to noon other and holy to stycke to theme as true and faithfull subjectes ought to doo to their regall rulers governours and supreame heddes; and for sure corroboracion therof be it further enacted by auctoritie aforsaid that suche persone and persones as to whom it shall please your majestie to dispose lymyt and assigne the said dominion of this lande and other the premisses therunto apperteynyng by your lettres patentes or by your last will as is aforsaid shall have and enyoic the same after your deceasse and for lacke of heyres of your
