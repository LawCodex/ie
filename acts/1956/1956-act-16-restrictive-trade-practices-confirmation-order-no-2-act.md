# Restrictive Trade Practices (Confirmation Order) (No. 2) Act, 1956

AN ACT TO CONFIRM THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES (BUILDING MATERIALS) ORDER, 1955, MADE BY THE MINISTER FOR INDUSTRY AND COMMERCE UNDER SECTION 9 OF THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES ACT, 1953. [26*th April,* 1956.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

## 1. Confirmation of Order.

**1.**—The Restrictive Trade Practices (Building Materials) Order, 1955 (S.I. No. 187 of 1955), made by the Minister for Industry and Commerce under section 9 of the Restrictive Trade Practices Act, 1953 (No. 14 of 1953), is hereby confirmed.

## 2. Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Restrictive Trade Practices (Confirmation of Order) (No. 2) Act, 1956.
