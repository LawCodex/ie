# Appropriation Act, 2001

AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE TO THE PROPER SUPPLY SERVICES AND PURPOSES SUMS GRANTED BY THE CENTRAL FUND (PERMANENT PROVISIONS) ACT, 1965, AND TO MAKE CERTAIN PROVISION IN RELATION TO THE FINANCIAL RESOLUTIONS, PASSED BY DÁIL ÉIREANN ON 5 DECEMBER 2001. [20*th December*, 2001]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

Appropriation of Grants

## 1. Appropriation of sums voted for supply services, viz., £20,362,692,000.

**1.**—(1) The sums granted by the Central Fund (Permanent Provisions) Act, 1965, out of the Central Fund towards making good the supply granted, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of twenty thousand, three hundred and sixty-two million, six hundred and ninety-two thousand pounds for the service of the year ending on 31 December 2001 are appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the *Schedule* to this Act.

(2) In addition to the grants out of the Central Fund referred to in *subsection (1)* of this section, there may be applied out of any money directed under section 2 of the Public Accounts and Charges Act, 1891, to be applied as appropriations-in-aid of the grants for the services and purposes specified in the *Schedule* to this Act, the sums respectively set out in *column (4)* of that Schedule amounting, as appears thereby, in the aggregate to the sum of one thousand, eight hundred and two million, four hundred and forty-four thousand pounds.

Miscellaneous

## 2. Financial resolutions passed by Dáil Éireann on 5 December 2001.

**2.**—(1) The legislation required to give effect to the financial resolutions passed by Dáil Éireann on 5 December 2001 and referred to in *subsection (2)* of this section may, subject to the time period specified in section 4 of the Provisional Collection of Taxes Act, 1927, be enacted in the year 2002.

(2) The Financial Resolutions referred to in *subsection (1)* of this section are as follows:

(*a*) Financial Resolution No. 1 (which relates to the taxation of life assurance policyholders),

(*b*) Financial Resolution No. 2 (which relates to excise duty on mineral oils),

(*c*) Financial Resolution No. 3 (which relates to excise duty on tobacco products),

(*d*) Financial Resolution No. 4 (which relates to excise duty on cider and perry), and

(*e*) Financial Resolution No. 5 (which relates to value-added tax).

## 3. Short title.

**3.**—This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 2001.
