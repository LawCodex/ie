# National Tourism Development Authority (Amendment) Act 2022

An Act to increase the aggregate of moneys that the National Tourism Development Authority may advance to enterprises and projects to which section 8(1)(e) of the National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003 applies and for that purpose to amend section 24 of the National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003.

[21*st December,* 2022]

**Be it enacted by the Oireachtas as follows:**

## 1. Amendment of section 24 of National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003

**1.** Section 24 of the National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003 is amended, in subsection (2), by the substitution of “€500,000,000” for “€300,000,000”.

## 2. Short title

**2.** This Act may be cited as the National Tourism Development Authority (Amendment) Act 2022.
