# Appropriation Act, 2000

AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE TO THE PROPER SUPPLY SERVICES AND PURPOSES SUMS GRANTED BY THE CENTRAL FUND (PERMANENT PROVISIONS) ACT, 1965, AND TO MAKE CERTAIN PROVISION IN RELATION TO THE FINANCIAL RESOLUTIONS, PASSED BY DÁIL ÉIREANN ON 6 DECEMBER 2000. [15*th December*, 2000]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

Appropriation of Grants

## 1. Appropriation of sums voted for supply services, viz., £16,864,443,000.

**1.**—(1) The sums granted by the Central Fund (Permanent Provisions) Act, 1965, out of the Central Fund towards making good the supply granted, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of sixteen thousand, eight hundred and sixty-four million, four hundred and forty-three thousand pounds for the service of the year ending on 31 December 2000 are appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the *Schedule* hereto.

(2) In addition to the grants out of the Central Fund referred to in the foregoing subsection of this section, there may be applied out of any money directed under section 2 of the Public Accounts and Charges Act, 1891, to be applied as appropriations-in-aid of the grants for the services and purposes specified in the *Schedule* hereto, the sums respectively set forth in *column (4)* of the said Schedule amounting, as appears thereby, in the aggregate to the sum of one thousand, seven hundred and eighty-nine million, five hundred and fifty-seven thousand pounds.

Miscellaneous

## 2. Financial resolutions passed by Dáil Éireann on 6 December 2000.

**2.**—(1) The legislation required to give effect to the financial resolutions passed by Dáil Éireann on 6 December 2000 and referred to in *subsection (2)* of this section may, subject to the time period specified in section 4 of the Provisional Collection of Taxes Act, 1927, be enacted in the year 2001.

(2) The Financial Resolutions referred to in *subsection (1)* of this section are as follows:

(*a*) Financial Resolution No. 1 (which relates to excise duty on hydrocarbons and substitute motor fuel),

(*b*) Financial Resolution No. 2 (which relates to excise duty on tobacco products), and

(*c*) Financial Resolution No. 3 (which relates to value-added tax).

## 3. Short title.

**3.**—This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 2000.
