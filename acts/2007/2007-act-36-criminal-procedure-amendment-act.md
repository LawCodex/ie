# Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2007

AN ACT TO AMEND THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE ACT 1967.

[25*th October*, 2007]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

## 1. Amendment of Criminal Procedure Act 1967.

**1**.— Section 22 of the Criminal Procedure Act 1967 is amended by—

(*a*) the substitution of the following subsection for subsection (3) (inserted by section 18(*b*) of the Criminal Justice Act 2007):

“(3) A recognisance to which subsection (2) applies may be taken by—

(*a*) any judge of the District Court,

(*b*) any District Court clerk,

(*c*) a peace commissioner designated for that purpose by order of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform,

(*d*) the governor of a prison, or

(*e*) a prison officer designated for that purpose by the governor of a prison.”,

and

(*b*) the insertion of the following subsection:

“(5) In this section ‘ prison ’ has the same meaning as it has in the Prisons Act 2007.”.

## 2. Short title.

**2**.— This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act 2007.
