# Appropriation Act, 1990

AN ACT TO APPLY A CERTAIN SUM OUT OF THE CENTRAL FUND TO THE SERVICE OF THE YEAR ENDED ON THE 31ST DAY OF DECEMBER, 1987, AND TO APPROPRIATE TO THE PROPER SUPPLY SERVICES AND PURPOSES SUMS GRANTED BY THE CENTRAL FUND (PERMANENT PROVISIONS) ACT, 1965, AND THIS ACT. [26*th December*, 1990]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

Grant out of the Central Fund

## 1. Issue of £1,000 out of the Central Fund for the year 1987.

**1.**—The Minister for Finance may issue out of the Central Fund and apply towards making good the supply granted for the service of the year ended on the 31st day of December, 1987, the sum of one thousand pounds.

Appropriation of Grants

## 2. Appropriation of sums voted for, supply services, viz., £6,620,550,000.

**2.**—(1) The sums granted by the Central Fund (Permanent Provisions) Act, 1965, and this Act out of the Central Fund towards making good the supply granted, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of six thousand, six hundred and twenty million, five hundred and fifty thousand pounds and comprising the sum of one thousand pounds for the service of the year ended on the 31st day of December, 1987, and the sum of six thousand, six hundred and twenty million, five hundred and forty-nine thousand pounds for the service of the year ending on the 31st day of December, 1990, are appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the Schedule hereto.

(2) In addition to the grants out of the Central Fund referred to in the foregoing subsection of this section, there may be applied out of any money directed under section 2 of the Public Accounts and Charges Act, 1891, to be applied as appropriations-in-aid of the grants for the services and purposes specified in the Schedule hereto, the sums respectively set forth in *column (4)* of the said Schedule amounting, as appears thereby, in the aggregate to the sum of seven hundred and thirteen million, eight hundred and eighty-seven thousand, seven hundred and forty-nine pounds.

## 3. Short title.

**3.**—This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 1990.
