# Appropriation Act 2014

An Act to appropriate to the proper supply services and purposes sums granted by the Central Fund (Permanent Provisions) Act 1965, to make provision in relation to deferred surrender to the Central Fund of certain undischarged appropriations by reference to the capital supply services and purposes as provided for by section 91 of the Finance Act 2004 and, for the purpose of maintaining a sufficient amount of moneys in the Paymaster General’s supply account so as to enable the discharge of particular liabilities, to make provision for repayable advances from the Central Fund, and to provide for related matters.

[19 *th December*, 2014]

**Be it enacted by the Oireachtas as follows:**

## 1. Appropriation of sums voted for supply services, totalling €42,657,904,000

**1.** (1) The sums granted by the Central Fund (Permanent Provisions) Act 1965, out of the Central Fund towards making good the supply granted, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of forty-two thousand, six hundred and fifty-seven million, nine hundred and four thousand euro for the service of the year ending on 31 December 2014 are appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in *Schedule 1*.

(2) In addition to the grants out of the Central Fund referred to in *subsection (1)*, there may be applied out of any money directed under section 2 of the Public Accounts and Charges Act 1891 to be applied as appropriations-in-aid of the grants for the services and purposes specified in *Schedule 1*, the sums respectively set out in the last column of that Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the sum of three thousand, five hundred and eighty two million, seven hundred and eight thousand euro.

## 2. Deferred surrender relating to capital supply services, totalling €79,044,000

**2.** For the purposes of section 91 of the Finance Act 2004, in respect of certain undischarged appropriations by reference to the capital supply services and purposes included in a vote referred to in *Schedule 1*, there are set out in *Schedule 2* the sums deferred into the year ending 31 December 2015, and the related votes and titles in respect of each of those sums, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of seventy nine million and forty four thousand euro.

## 3. Repayable advances from the Central Fund

**3.** (1) The Minister for Finance may, following a request from the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, and for the purpose of maintaining a sufficient amount of moneys in the Paymaster General’s Supply Account, having regard to the sums payable from that Account, put in place funding arrangements to facilitate electronic funds transfers where payments to discharge a liability are due on 1 and 2 January 2015.

(2) Amounts required by the Minister for Finance under *subsection (1)*, which in aggregate shall not exceed €250 million, shall be advanced out of the Central Fund or the growing produce thereof.

(3) Any sums so advanced shall be repaid from the Paymaster General’s Supply Account to the Central Fund in January 2015.

## 4. Amendment of section 3 of Appropriation Act 1999

**4.** Section 3 of the Appropriation Act 1999 is amended with effect from 1 January 2015—

(a) by substituting “1 January 2015” for “1 January 2006”,

(b) by substituting “Minister for Health” for “Health Service Executive” in both places where it occurs, and

(c) by substituting “his or her functions” for “its functions”.

## 5. Short title

**5.** This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act 2014.
