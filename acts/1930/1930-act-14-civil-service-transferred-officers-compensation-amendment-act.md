# Civil Service (Transferred Officers) Compensation (Amendment) Act, 1930

AN ACT TO AMEND THE CIVIL SERVICE (TRANSFERRED OFFICERS) COMPENSATION ACT, 1929, BY EXTENDING THE LIMIT IMPOSED BY SECTION 15 OF THAT ACT ON THE DATE OF RETIREMENT OF CERTAIN TRANSFERRED OFFICERS. [4*th June*, 1930.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS OF SAORSTAT EIREANN AS FOLLOWS:—

## 1. The Principal Act.

**1.**—In this Act the expression “the Principal Act” means the Civil Service (Transferred Officers) Compensation Act, 1929 (No. 36 of 1929).

## 2. Amendment of Section 15 of the Principal Act.

**2.**—Sub-section (2) of Section 15 of the Principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution of the words “six months” for the words “four months” in paragraph (*a*) and also in paragraph (*b*) of that sub-section, and the said Section 15 shall be construed and have effect accordingly.

## 3. Short title and citation.

**3.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Civil Service (Transferred Officers) Compensation (Amendment) Act, 1930.

(2) The Principal Act and this Act may be cited together as the Civil Service (Transferred Officers) Compensation Acts, 1929 and 1930.
