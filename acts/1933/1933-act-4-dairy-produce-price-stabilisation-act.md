# Dairy Produce (Price Stabilisation) Act, 1933

AN ACT TO AMEND THE DAIRY PRODUCE (PRICE STABILISATION) ACT, 1932. [13*th April,* 1933.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS OF SAORSTÁT EIREANN AS FOLLOWS:—

## 1. The Principal Act.

**1.**—In this Act the expression “the Principal Act” means the Dairy Produce (Price Stabilisation) Act, 1932 (No. 10 of 1932).

## 2. Amendments of the Principal Act.

**2.**—The Principal Act shall be construed and have effect as if the sections and sub-sections thereof mentioned in the first column of the Schedule to this Act were respectively amended in the manner set out in the second column of that Schedule opposite each such section and sub-section.

## 3. Short title and citation.

**3.**—This Act may be cited as the Dairy Produce (Price Stabilisation) Act, 1933, and the Principal Act and this Act may be cited together as the Dairy Produce (Price Stabilisation) Acts, 1932 and 1933.
