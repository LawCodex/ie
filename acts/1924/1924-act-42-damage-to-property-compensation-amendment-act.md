# Damage To Property (Compensation) (Amendment) Act, 1924

AN ACT TO AUTHORISE THE ISSUE OF SECURITIES IN CASES WITHIN SECTION 15 OF THE DAMAGE TO PROPERTY (COMPENSATION) ACT, 1923. [1*st August*, 1924.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS OF SAORSTÁT EIREANN AS FOLLOWS:—

## 1. Definition of “Principal Act.”

**1.**—In this Act the expression “the Principal Act” means the Damage to Property (Compensation) Act, 1923 (No. 15 of 1923).

## 2. Issue of securities in cases within section 15 of Principal Act.

**2.**—Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in section 13 of the Principal Act, the Minister for Finance may issue securities created by an order made under that section to applicants in cases within section 15 of the Principal Act in discharge of a sum not exceeding the sum mentioned in pursuance of paragraph (*b*) of sub-section (3) of the said section 15 in the report made by the Judge under that sub-section.

## 3. Short title and citation.

**3.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Damage to Property (Compensation) (Amendment) Act, 1924.

(2) The Damage to Property (Compensation) Act, 1923 (No. 15 of 1923), the Damage to Property (Amendment) Act, 1923 (No. 43 of 1923), and this Act may be cited together as the Damage to Property (Compensation) Acts, 1923 and 1924.
